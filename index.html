Our mission

We are on a mission to experience the Church of Jesus Christ as the Church Scripture describes. Alongside leaders around the world, we labor for mighty believers who are pure and majestic, awesome and immovable, powerful and world-changing, and to experience that Church once again living as Jesus said He would build it.

Believe what He promised.
Live as if He meant it.

Conviction

How we think

Jesus in first place, the Bible as the standard, and the Holy Spirit expected. Nothing we make gets to contradict it.

  1. 01

    Jesus has first place.

    In everything. We do not preach ourselves. A piece that puts a person, a method, or a mood ahead of Him does not go out.

  2. 02

    Believe the Bible, not our experience.

    The text is the standard. Our habits are not. When the New Testament treats a life as normal, we do not file it under a special season.

  3. 03

    Embrace and expect the Spirit.

    No shying away from the gifts and operation of the Holy Spirit. We embrace them. We expect them.

  4. 04

    That they would be one.

    Jesus is our peace. We do not sort believers into camps to grow a following. We help believers in local churches find one another in Him.

  5. 05

    No spectators.

    Every gift is recognized and valued. The work should help the members find their part, not collect a crowd around one voice.

  6. 06

    Set the Church free.

    Allow every member to do their part. The focus is on ministries, giftings, and responsibilities, not titles. The Church is meant to spread from life to life.

The work

What we make

Bible teaching, witness, and media for believers in local churches. Does this help someone know Jesus?

01

Teaching

Messages, series, and Bible study that believers in a local church can trust, return to, and put in someone else’s hands. Made to be used, not just watched.

02

Witness

Plain speech about Jesus, the authority of Scripture, and the Church He is building. Clear enough to follow. Solid enough to keep.

03

With churches

Media for the gatherings, training, and daily life of believers in local churches. Their voice, helped to carry, in a form believers in another church can receive.

If it helps the Church know Jesus, it belongs here. If it only helps us be seen, it does not.
