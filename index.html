Our mission

We earnestly desire to see the Church of Jesus Christ live the way Scripture describes her: pure, united, and mighty because of Jesus. With others who have this same hope and expectation, we seek to be a living example of that Church, and we make Bible teaching and training for believers, their homes, and their local churches. Jesus said He would build His Church. We take Him at His word.

Believe what He promised.
Live as if He meant it.

Conviction

How we think

Jesus in first place, the Bible as the standard, and the Holy Spirit expected, to the glory of God the Father.

  1. 01

    Jesus has first place.

    He is the head of the body, the Church, and in all things He has the preeminence. Jesus is the focus, source, and foundation of all truth. Colossians 1:18; John 14:6; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 3:11

  2. 02

    Believe the Bible, not our experience.

    Scripture is the inspired, inerrant, and authoritative Word of God. It reveals Jesus, and our lives are submitted to it. 2 Timothy 3:16-17; John 5:39; Hebrews 4:12; James 1:22

  3. 03

    Embrace and expect the Holy Spirit.

    The Holy Spirit glorifies Jesus. We welcome His working, we value every gift He gives, and we expect Him to move. We test everything by Scripture. John 16:14; 1 Corinthians 12:7-11; 1 Thessalonians 5:19-21

  4. 04

    Strengthen the home.

    God uses the family to build His Church: a godly marriage reflects Christ and the Church, and children are raised to serve the Lord. Every believer has a place, married or single. Ephesians 5:31-32; Malachi 2:15; Ephesians 6:4; Joshua 24:15; Ephesians 2:19

  5. 05

    That they would be one.

    Jesus died to make us one, and where brethren dwell together in unity, the Lord commands His blessing. When every opinion becomes a hill to die on, we get faction instead of peace. Our focus must be Jesus and His Word. Ephesians 2:14-16; Psalm 133; John 17:20-23; 1 Corinthians 1:10; Romans 14:1

  6. 06

    No spectators.

    The body of Christ is not designed for observation but for participation. Each member has a gift and a critical role to play. We need each other. 1 Corinthians 12:7; 1 Corinthians 12:14-22; 1 Peter 4:10; Ephesians 4:16

  7. 07

    Set the Church free.

    Christ gave leaders to equip the saints, not to contain or diminish them. Jesus designed the Church to thrive and to spread from life to life. In Him we are immovable and mighty, and we do not cower. Ephesians 4:11-12; Matthew 16:18; 1 Corinthians 15:58; 2 Timothy 1:7

Belief

What we believe

The foundation of everything we do.

  1. 01

    Jesus Christ

    Jesus is God the Son, fully God and fully man. He was born of a virgin, lived without sin, died for our sins, rose bodily, and sits at the Father’s right hand. He is the only way to the Father. John 1:1, 14; Matthew 1:23; Hebrews 4:15; 1 Corinthians 15:3-4; Hebrews 1:3; John 14:6

  2. 02

    The Bible

    The sixty-six books of the Bible are the Word of God, inspired by Him, without error, and preserved by Him. His Word is living and powerful, and it is our final authority. 2 Timothy 3:16-17; 2 Peter 1:20-21; Hebrews 4:12; Matthew 24:35

  3. 03

    How We Are Saved

    Every one of us has sinned and fallen short. We are saved by grace through faith in the blood of Christ, not by works but unto good works. We repent, we believe, and the Spirit makes us new. Romans 3:23; Ephesians 2:4-10; Mark 1:15; Romans 3:24-25; Titus 3:5; James 2:14-17

  4. 04

    Who God Is

    God is eternally Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Matthew 28:19; John 1:1-5; John 16:7-8

  5. 05

    The Holy Spirit

    Every believer is sealed with the Spirit and told to be filled with Him. He sets us apart from sin, gives power to live holy, and gives every member gifts for today, as He wills. Ephesians 1:13-14; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:3; Galatians 5:16; 1 Corinthians 12:7-11

  6. 06

    The Church

    The Church is the body of Christ and His bride, the fullness of Him who fills all in all. As every member does its part, the body builds itself up in love, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it. Ephesians 1:22-23; Ephesians 5:25-27; Ephesians 4:16; Matthew 16:18

  7. 07

    The Family

    The family is God’s design. He gives husbands, wives, and children each a calling, and all children are a blessing from Him. Ephesians 5:22-33; Ephesians 6:1-4; Psalm 127:3-5

  8. 08

    His Return

    Jesus is coming again to judge the living and the dead and to reign forever. Hell is real and eternal. So is the life He gives. Acts 1:11; 2 Timothy 4:1; Matthew 25:46; Revelation 11:15

The work

What we do

Bible teaching and training for believers, their homes, and their local churches, through messages, podcasts, and other resources. Everything we do has to answer one question. Does it help someone know Jesus?

01

Teaching

Messages, series, and Bible study that a father can open at home and a local church can trust, return to, and hand to someone else. Made to be used.

02

Training

Discipleship for believers, their homes, and their local churches. It teaches the life Scripture describes, and it is made to be practiced.

03

With churches

For the gatherings, training, and daily life of local churches, we help a church carry what God has given it to believers in other churches.

If it helps the Church know Jesus, it belongs here. If it only helps us be seen, it does not.
