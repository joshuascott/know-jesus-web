Our mission

We earnestly desire to see the Church of Jesus Christ live the way Scripture describes her: pure, united, and mighty because of Jesus. With others who have this same hope and expectation, we seek to be a living example of that Church, and we make Bible teaching and training for believers, their homes, and their local churches. Jesus said He would build His Church. We take Him at His word.

Believe what He promised.
Live as if He meant it.

Conviction

How we think

Jesus in first place, the Bible as the standard, and the Holy Spirit expected, to the glory of God the Father. Nothing we make gets to contradict that.

  1. 01

    Jesus has first place.

    In everything (Colossians 1:18). We do not preach ourselves (2 Corinthians 4:5). If a piece puts a person or a method ahead of Him, it does not go out.

  2. 02

    Believe the Bible, not our experience.

    Scripture is inspired and without error. It is the standard, and our experience is measured by it (2 Timothy 3:16; Acts 17:11). When the New Testament treats a life as normal, we do not file it under a special season (Acts 2:42-47).

  3. 03

    Embrace and expect the Holy Spirit.

    He is a Person to be obeyed, and His work is to glorify Jesus (John 16:14). We do not shy away from His gifts. We embrace them and expect them. We are not charismatic or Pentecostal in the denominational sense, and Scripture tests everything (1 Thessalonians 5:19-21).

  4. 04

    Strengthen the home.

    After Jesus, the home comes first. The Church is strengthened when fathers and mothers are strengthened, so we make teaching that helps a father lead his home, a mother build hers, and both bring up their children in the Lord (Ephesians 5:22-33; Ephesians 6:1-4; Deuteronomy 6:6-7; Titus 2:1-8). A man learns to care for the church of God in his own house (1 Timothy 3:4-5). Children are welcome among us, as they were with Jesus (Matthew 19:14). The household of God has a place for every believer, married or single (Ephesians 2:19; 1 Corinthians 7:32-35).

  5. 05

    That they would be one.

    Jesus is our peace (Ephesians 2:14). He made us one, and we are told to keep it (John 17:20-23; Ephesians 4:1-6). We do not sort believers into camps to grow a following. We help believers in local churches find one another in Him, around what Scripture makes plain.

  6. 06

    No spectators.

    Every member functions, and the pastor is one of them (1 Corinthians 12:14-22; Ephesians 4:16). Every gift is recognized and valued. What we make should help members find their part. It should never gather a crowd around one voice.

  7. 07

    Set the Church free.

    Christ gave leaders to equip the saints for the work of ministry (Ephesians 4:11-12), so we keep control light. Ministries, giftings, and responsibilities matter more to us than titles. The Church is meant to spread from life to life and to keep going without us.

Belief

What we believe

The foundation under everything else on this page.

  1. 01

    Jesus Christ

    Jesus is God the Son, fully God and fully man. He was born of a virgin, lived without sin, died for our sins, rose bodily, and sits at the Father’s right hand. He is the only way to the Father (John 1:1, 14; Matthew 1:23; Hebrews 4:15; 1 Corinthians 15:3-4; Hebrews 1:3; John 14:6).

  2. 02

    How We Are Saved

    God made man upright. Man sinned, and every one of us stands guilty before Him. We are saved by grace through faith in the blood of Christ, not by works but unto good works. We repent, we believe, and the Spirit makes us new. Living faith works (Ecclesiastes 7:29; Romans 3:23; Ephesians 2:4-10; Mark 1:15; Romans 3:24-25; Titus 3:5; James 2:14-17).

  3. 03

    Who God Is

    God is eternally Father, Son, and Holy Spirit (Matthew 28:19; John 1:1-5; John 16:7-8).

  4. 04

    The Holy Spirit

    Every believer is sealed with the Spirit and told to be filled with Him. He sets us apart from sin unto God, gives the power to live holy, and gives gifts to every member as He wills. All of them are for today (Ephesians 1:13-14; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:3; Galatians 5:16; 1 Corinthians 12:7-11).

  5. 05

    The Church

    The Church is the body of Christ, His representation on the earth, and every member has a function (1 Corinthians 12:27; Ephesians 1:22-23; Ephesians 4:15-16).

  6. 06

    The Family

    The family is God’s design. He gives husbands, wives, and children each a calling, and children are a heritage from Him (Ephesians 5:22-33; Ephesians 6:1-4; Psalm 127:3-5).

  7. 07

    His Return

    Jesus is coming again to judge the living and the dead and to reign forever. Hell is real and eternal. So is the life He gives (Acts 1:11; 2 Timothy 4:1; Matthew 25:46; Revelation 11:15).

The work

What we do

Bible teaching and training for believers, their homes, and their local churches, through messages, podcasts, and other resources. Everything we do has to answer one question. Does it help someone know Jesus?

01

Teaching

Messages, series, and Bible study that a father can open at home and a local church can trust, return to, and hand to someone else. Made to be used.

02

Training

Discipleship for believers, their homes, and their local churches. It teaches the life Scripture describes, and it is made to be practiced.

03

With churches

For the gatherings, training, and daily life of local churches, we help a church carry what God has given it to believers in other churches.

If it helps the Church know Jesus, it belongs here. If it only helps us be seen, it does not.
